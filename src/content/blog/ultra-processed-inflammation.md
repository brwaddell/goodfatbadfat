Ultra-processed foods are the ones built in a factory with long ingredient lists — sodas, many chips, packaged sweets, and some frozen meals. They are easy. They are also linked with more inflammation in the body.

## What the research found

In the Melbourne Collaborative Cohort Study, adults who ate more ultra-processed food (using the **NOVA** system) had **higher hs-CRP**, a sensitive inflammation marker. Part of that link stayed even after accounting for body weight.

Other large studies, including work from Brazil (ELSA-Brasil) and U.S. survey data, show a similar pattern: more ultra-processed food, more signs of inflammation.

## What this means for you

You do not need a perfect pantry. Start with one swap — a home-cooked fish dinner instead of a deep-fried takeout meal. GoodFat looks at processing signals when it can, so those swaps are easier to see.
