When your body stays inflamed, you may feel tired, sore, or foggy. Omega‑3 fats from fish — called **EPA** and **DHA** — help your body turn that “on” signal down.

## What the research found

An umbrella review of many meta-analyses found that omega‑3 supplements lowered three common blood markers of inflammation:

- **CRP** (C-reactive protein)
- **TNF-α**
- **IL-6**

In simple terms: more calming fats, less “fire” in the blood for many adults who were studied.

## What this means for you

You do not need to be perfect. Adding fatty fish a few times a week — or asking your clinician about omega‑3 — can be a gentle step toward feeling better.
