Feeling better today matters. So does how you age. Large studies suggest that **patterns rich in good fats** — olive oil, nuts, fish, plants — support a longer, healthier life.

## What the research found

The **PREDIMED** trial followed more than 7,000 adults at high heart risk. People who ate a Mediterranean-style diet with extra-virgin olive oil or nuts had **fewer major heart events** than people advised to eat lower fat.

In related PREDIMED analyses, higher olive oil intake was also linked with lower heart disease risk.

## What this means for you

Cook with olive oil. Snack on a small handful of nuts. Add fish when you can. These are not “cheat” foods — they are tools for staying well.
