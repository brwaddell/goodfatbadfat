Inflammation does not only live in your joints. It can also show up in your mood — fog, heaviness, or feeling low for no clear reason.

Omega‑3 fats, especially **EPA**, have been studied as a support for people with depression.

## What the research found

Meta-analyses of clinical trials suggest that **EPA-rich** omega‑3 formulas (often about 1–2 grams of EPA per day, with EPA making up most of the blend) can ease depressive symptoms for some people with clinical depression.

Guidelines from nutritional psychiatry researchers also highlight EPA-forward formulas as the best-studied option.

This is **not** a replacement for care from a doctor or therapist. It is one piece of the picture.

## What this means for you

If mood is a concern, talk with your clinician. Meanwhile, choosing more seafood and fewer heavy fry oils is a gentle daily habit that may support how you feel.
